BRIEF JOB SUMMARY

The Human Resource (HR) Associate initiates, coordinates, and organizes human resource functions and related activities for the Department of Disability and Human Development (DHD). These functions include establishing priorities and coordinating the personnel needs of diverse departmental activities. The HR Associate functions as a consultant to various constituencies within the department, reviewing and providing guidance and/or problem resolution for highly sensitive and unique human resource-related matters.


 


Duties and Responsibilities - Civil Service Campus

Duties are listed in order of greatest importance.  Other responsibilities may be assigned.

Oversees, monitors and coordinates day-to-day HR services and transactions for DHD, ensuring compliance with university policies, procedures, regulations, and standards of practice; processes all HR related transactions utilizing university systems, e.g. Banner, HR Front End, HireTouch, Cornerstone, JDXpert, ANA, BANNER, PARIS, Rehire Retiree, Salary Planner, I9 Tracker, etc.

Responsible for the payroll functions for employees within the department utilizing University HR/Pay systems, including PARIS, Webtime, etc - payroll functions include, but are not limited to, approving bi-weekly time sheets in WebTime entry, payroll adjustments, processing merit increases, lump sum payments, stipends, overpayments, change of pay status, etc; advises managers on payroll policies and the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA).

5%
