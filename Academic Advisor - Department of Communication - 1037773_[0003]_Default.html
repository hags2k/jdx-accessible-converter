PRIMARY POSITION FUNCTION/SUMMARY

Academic Advisors in the Department of Communication provide holistic academic advising and coordination of student services in support of the personal, educational, and professional development of undergraduate students. Advisors serve as the primary point of contact for prospective and admitted students, offering guidance on academic planning, degree progress, experiential learning opportunities, and post‑baccalaureate pathways. Advisors collaborate closely with faculty and departmental leadership to ensure students receive accurate, timely, and developmentally appropriate support throughout their academic careers.
